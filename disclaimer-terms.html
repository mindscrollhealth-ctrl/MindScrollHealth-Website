1. Nature of MindScroll Health

MindScroll Health is a psychoeducational organization that translates peer-reviewed research on social media and mental health into accessible tools and resources. Our scoping review covers 79 sources published between 2000 and 2025 and is registered on the Open Science Framework.

MindScroll Health is not a licensed mental health provider, clinical practice, therapy service, crisis intervention service, or medical organization. No individual affiliated with MindScroll Health acts in a clinical capacity through the provision of our materials.

Our resources are educational in nature. They are intended to support awareness, reflection, and informed decision-making. They are not designed to diagnose, treat, or manage any mental health condition.

2. Not Medical or Clinical Advice

Nothing provided by MindScroll Health constitutes:

  • Medical advice of any kind
  • A clinical mental health assessment or evaluation
  • A diagnosis of any mental health condition, disorder, or illness
  • A treatment recommendation or therapeutic intervention
  • A prescription or clinical referral
  • Crisis counseling or emergency mental health support

This applies to all MindScroll materials, including assessments (Passive vs. Active Use, General Wellbeing, Post-Use Check-In), the Usage Pattern Tracker, the Sleep and Phone Check-In, the research explainer, presentation content, website content, and email communications.

The assessments and feedback MindScroll provides are psychoeducational tools grounded in peer-reviewed research. They are designed to surface patterns and provide general information relevant to those patterns. They do not evaluate any individual's clinical status.

Even when MindScroll content draws directly from peer-reviewed research, that research describes associations found across populations. Association is not diagnosis. Population-level findings do not determine individual outcomes. Our feedback is based on what the literature identifies at the group level and cannot account for the full complexity of any individual's circumstances.

3. Research Claims and Accuracy

MindScroll materials reference and summarize peer-reviewed research. We make reasonable efforts to represent this research accurately. However:

  • The evidence base on social media and mental health is evolving. Findings may be updated, revised, or superseded by future research.
  • Most effect sizes reported in the literature are small. MindScroll does not overstate the magnitude of any association.
  • Correlational findings (which constitute most of the evidence base) describe associations, not causal relationships. MindScroll uses correlational language accordingly.
  • MindScroll Health does not claim authorship of cited research and does not represent that cited researchers endorse MindScroll Health or its materials.
  • Summary and interpretation of research findings is the responsibility of MindScroll Health alone and may not reflect the full conclusions or nuance of original sources.

Users are encouraged to consult original research where questions arise.

What MindScroll does not do with your data

  • We do not sell data to third parties.
  • We do not share assessment responses, tracker data, or personal information with schools, healthcare providers, insurers, law enforcement, or any external organization without explicit consent.
  • We do not use data for advertising or commercial purposes.
  • We do not create user profiles or track individuals across sessions (except for enrolled study participants who voluntarily submit scores linked to anonymous study IDs).

Formsubmit (third-party form service)

Forms on this site use Formsubmit, a third-party service that delivers form submissions to our email. Formsubmit processes data in transit to deliver it to us. MindScroll Health has reviewed Formsubmit's practices and determined them appropriate for the type of data collected (non-clinical, non-financial). For questions about Formsubmit's data handling, refer to their privacy policy at formsubmit.co.

Data retention

  • Browser-based data (assessments, tracker, sleep check-in): retained on your device until you clear your browser data. MindScroll never has access to it.
  • Form submissions (contact, application, study enrollment): retained in MindScroll's email system for as long as needed to respond to the inquiry or manage the study. Deleted when no longer needed.
  • Longitudinal study data: retained for the duration of the study and any subsequent analysis or publication period. Study data is anonymous (linked to study IDs, not personal identifiers).
  • Google Analytics data: retained according to Google's default retention settings (14 months). MindScroll does not extend this period.

7. If You Are in Crisis

MindScroll is not a crisis resource. If you or someone you know is experiencing thoughts of suicide or self-harm, is in immediate danger, or is experiencing a mental health emergency, do not rely on MindScroll materials. Contact one of the following immediately:

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: Call or text 988 (United States)
Crisis Text Line: Text HOME to 741741
Emergency services: Call 911 or go to your nearest emergency room
School counselor or trusted adult in your immediate environment
